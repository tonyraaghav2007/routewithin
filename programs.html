Can I join if my partner refuses to participate?

Yes! Over 60% of our successful alumni began the journey alone. In the Anter Prakash philosophy, when one partner fundamentally shifts their internal reactions, defensiveness, and verbal cadence, the relational dynamic inevitably transforms.

How does the Saturday live Zoom cohort work?

Sessions run every Saturday from 4:30 PM to 5:30 PM IST. You will join via Zoom Live, receive live teachings and diagnostic exercises from Dimple Sandhu, and have opportunities to ask confidential questions during the session.

What makes NLP alignment different from standard couples therapy?

Traditional therapy often focuses on talking repeatedly through the past conflict. NLP alignment works on the subconscious sensory triggers and neurological anchors that create anger or panic in milliseconds, empowering you to stop reactivity before it starts.
