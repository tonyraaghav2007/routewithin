MY STORY & CREDENTIALS

Dimple Sandhu

Certified Life & Relationship Coach • Master NLP

I created RouteWithin because I've seen how overwhelming and lonely relationships can feel. Through my own experiences, over 1,000+ lives touched, and years of coaching seekers, I realized how powerful the right guidance and perspective can be.

In Anter Prakash, you discover that you do not experience other people; you experience your own thinking about other people. We often wait until emotional burnout before asking for support. RouteWithin offers a safe sanctuary to pause, unpack subconscious loops, and build genuine emotional strength.
